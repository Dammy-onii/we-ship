# Competitive Research

A scan of existing couple / closeness apps, what they do well, and what Weship borrows or avoids. Sources at the bottom.

## The landscape

| App | Core idea | What's strong | What we take |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Locket Widget** | Photos pushed straight to a friend's home-screen widget | The surprise loop — a new photo every unlock. Went viral from one TikTok. | Evidence that a focused home-screen surface can matter; Weship currently keeps its widget to the top incomplete 2do |
| **Paired** | Daily prompts / questions for couples, expert-backed | Daily-question habit; App Store "App of the Day" | Daily Question as one hook — but not the *only* one |
| **Cupla** | Shared calendar for couples | Countdown widget to next visit; shared to-dos; quick check-in widgets | Countdown to next visit; light shared planning |
| **Between** | Private space for memories & special dates | Anniversary/date countdowns; stickers; "thinking of you" | Memories timeline; "thinking of you" nudges |
| **Couple2** | Private space + love widgets | Distance widget, real-time location, love widgets | Distance/timezone presence (location optional, privacy-gated) |
| **PairHabit / HabitBox / Flamme** | Habit tracking for two | Shared habits, nudge notifications, celebrate milestones | The **shared log / habit tracker** as a core retention engine |

## What the market says works (retention)

Consistent themes across write-ups:

- **A daily reason to open** — prompts, check-ins, or streaks that build a habit.
- **A co-owned shared space** — private journal, album, or calendar both partners own drives retention.
- **Gamification** — streaks, badges, rewards, quizzes reinforce bonding behavior.
- **Mood & wellness tracking** — mood logs, empathy, guided exercises keep partners aligned.
- **Shared habit tracking** — track independently, nudge each other, celebrate milestones together.

## What we deliberately avoid

- **Engagement-maximizing feeds / infinite scroll** — clashes with "calm."
- **Guilt-based streaks** — pressure hurts real relationships; keep streaks gentle and forgiving.
- **Always-on location by default** — privacy-gated and opt-in only.
- **Feature bloat / many tabs** — the market's weakness is doing too much. We stay at three tabs.
- **Heavy journaling** — favor tiny, tappable touchpoints over blank-page writing.

## Gaps we can own

1. **Our Take** — a flexible home for interactive in-camera scene packs for couples, beginning with a ranking format.
2. **A focused shared-task widget** — the current surface shows only the next incomplete 2do and deep-links to the full shared list.
3. **Calm neutral aesthetic** — the category skews pink/hearts/loud. Warm paper, graphite, quiet photography, and restrained accents are differentiated without tinting the app green.
