# Vision

## What Weship is

A shared private space for two people in a relationship — a "home" the two of you own together — plus a lighter social layer for friends. It supports both:

- **Together mode** — same city/home. Emphasis on shared planning, memories, in-person activities.
- **Long-distance mode** — emphasis on presence, timezones, "thinking of you" nudges, live widgets.

The app quietly adapts to distance and timezone instead of being two separate products.

## Who it's for

- Couples who want one calm place that is *theirs*, away from the noise of group chats and social feeds.
- Long-distance couples who need to feel present in each other's day.
- People who like small, low-pressure daily touchpoints over heavy journaling.

## What makes it different

1. **Useful home-screen presence.** The first widget stays intentionally narrow: the user’s top incomplete shared 2do, with a tap back into the full list. Rich partner-controlled widgets are not part of the current scope.
2. **Our Take.** Interactive in-camera scene packs—ranking, reactions, choices, and other prompts play *while you record*, TikTok/Snapchat style—not as a static face filter.
3. **Shared tasks, not project management.** 2do keeps the small things visible to both people, with gentle accountability.
4. **Calm by design.** Neutral, tactile, soft, and unhurried. The opposite of an engagement-maximizing social feed — closeness, not dopamine.

The app is called **Weship** — "we" + the ship you're sailing together, and the "ship" in relationship.

## Design principles

1. **Closeness first.** Every screen should make the other person feel present. Default to "us," not "me."
2. **Calm over busy.** Generous space, soft motion, and a warm neutral palette. Never more than a few choices on screen.
3. **Simple navigation.** Three bottom tabs, forever. Secondary things live behind the avatar, not in the tab bar.
4. **Low pressure.** Streaks encourage, they don't punish. Missing a day is forgiven gracefully.
5. **Private by default.** The couple Space is sacred and never leaks into the friends layer.
6. **Async-friendly.** Nothing important requires both people online at once.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Not a full messaging app — light notes and nudges, not a chat competitor.
- Not a public social network — friends are a small, private circle.
- Not a calendar/productivity suite — planning is light, feelings-first.

## Naming

The product is named **Weship** — "we" + "ship" (as in relationship, and a shared vessel you steer together).
